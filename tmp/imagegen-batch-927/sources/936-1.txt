S. (Bryan Stanley), 1933 - 1973 Title The unfortunates / by B.S. Johnson. Publication, distribution, etc. [London] : Panther Books in association with Secker & Warburg, 1969. Physical description 27 sections ([136] p.) in case ; 22 cm Note "This novel has twenty-seven sections, temporarily held together by a removable wrapper. Apart from the first and last sections ... the other twenty-five sections are intended to be read in random order."--Note on inside of case. Summary, etc. Scope and content: Example of a book for the active reader. A semi-autobiographical narrative where the novel is written as discrete sections. These sections, with the exception of the first and last, may be read in any order to create a random order of memory and experience in which a journalists returns to Nottingham to report on a football match but recalls and relives many former experiences in the processs. Provenance Inscribed by the author inside the lid of the box: "For Keith Botsford, towards a writers" co-operative, Bryan, 11/7/70", and also signed B.S. Johnson over the title. The recipient was an Anglo-American novelist associated with Saul Bellow. Subject English fiction 20th century. Subject Experimental fiction, English. Genre/Form Unbound sheets.
