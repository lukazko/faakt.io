Includes attached diagrams, demonstrating the progression of this photographic technique in producing a photographic image. The technique was designed by Douglas Trumbull (Special Effects Supervisor on 2001: A Space Odyssey) for filming the 'stargate' sequence of the film. The document is marked 'File belongs to Douglas Trumbull'.
